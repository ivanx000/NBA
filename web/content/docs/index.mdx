This site predicts who wins every NBA game, publishes the prediction before tip-off, and grades itself
once the game ends. These pages explain how, for anyone who wants the details: the data, the
features, the model, how it's checked, and where it falls short.

## What it predicts

For each regular-season game: the probability that the home team wins, and the handful of reasons
behind that number (for example "Celtics better rested" or "Knicks in better recent form").
Each team's top 8 players also get projected minutes, points, rebounds and assists with 80% ranges
([Player projections](/docs/player-projections)).

## How good it is

Honest numbers first. On the 2025-26 season, predicted month by month by models trained only on
earlier games (log loss rewards confident right calls and punishes confident wrong ones; lower is
better):

| Model | Accuracy | Log loss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always pick the home team | 55.5% | 0.688 |
| Elo ratings | 69.0% | 0.598 |
| **The model** (Elo-anchored, calibrated) | 67.9% | **0.595** |

The model beats picking the home team by a wide margin and beats Elo, a strong rating system, on
log loss, though Elo picked a few more winners that season. Across the six seasons before it, the
model beat Elo every time ([Models](/docs/models)). The [performance page](/performance) grades
both every day. Good NBA game models land in the mid to high 60s for accuracy; the site doesn't
promise more.

## How it fits together

1. **Data:** box scores and schedules from NBA.com, cached on disk ([Data sources](/docs/data-sources)).
2. **Features:** 57 numbers per game, each computed only from games before it ([Features](/docs/features), [Leakage](/docs/leakage)).
3. **Model:** Elo plus learned adjustments, calibrated, chosen against logistic regression, LightGBM and an ensemble on six held-out seasons ([Models](/docs/models), [Calibration](/docs/calibration)).
4. **Explanations:** each prediction split exactly into 12 human factors ([Explaining predictions](/docs/explaining-predictions)).
5. **Production:** a GitHub Actions job predicts each morning and writes to Postgres; the website only reads ([Daily pipeline](/docs/pipeline), [Architecture](/docs/architecture)).

> These predictions are not betting advice.
