Tip-Off predicts who wins every NBA game, publishes the prediction before tip-off, and grades itself
once the game ends. These pages explain how, for anyone who wants the details: the data, the
features, the model, how it's checked, and where it falls short.

## What it predicts

For each regular-season game: the probability that the home team wins, and the handful of reasons
behind that number (for example "Celtics better rested" or "Knicks in better recent form").
Player stat lines (points, rebounds and assists) are next on the roadmap.

## How good it is

Honest numbers first. On the 2025-26 season, which was held out of every decision and scored once:

| Model | Accuracy | Log loss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always pick the home team | 55.5% | 0.687 |
| Elo ratings | 69.2% | 0.598 |
| **Tip-Off model** (logistic regression, calibrated) | 67.1% | 0.602 |

The model clearly beats picking the home team, and it's close to Elo, a simple rating system, but
behind it on that season. The gap isn't statistically meaningful, and the model led Elo on the
validation season, so it stays the champion while Elo is tracked as the challenger on live games.
The [performance page](/performance) grades both every day. Good NBA game models land in the mid
to high 60s for accuracy; the site doesn't promise more.

## How it fits together

1. **Data:** box scores and schedules from NBA.com, cached on disk ([Data sources](/docs/data-sources)).
2. **Features:** 56 numbers per game, each computed only from games before it ([Features](/docs/features), [Leakage](/docs/leakage)).
3. **Model:** a calibrated logistic regression, chosen against Elo, LightGBM and an ensemble on a validation season ([Models](/docs/models), [Calibration](/docs/calibration)).
4. **Explanations:** each prediction split exactly into 11 human factors ([Explaining predictions](/docs/explaining-predictions)).
5. **Production:** a GitHub Actions job predicts each morning and writes to Postgres; the website only reads ([Daily pipeline](/docs/pipeline), [Architecture](/docs/architecture)).

> Tip-Off is an unofficial fan project. It isn't affiliated with the NBA, and its predictions are
> not betting advice.
