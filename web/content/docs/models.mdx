## How models were compared

- **Splits by time:** train on 2015-16 to 2023-24 (10,749 games), tune and calibrate on 2024-25,
  and test once on 2025-26. Nothing is ever chosen on test numbers.
- **Primary metric:** log loss, because the site shows probabilities, not just picks. Brier score,
  accuracy, AUC and calibration error are reported too, always next to the baselines.
- Every run is logged to MLflow with its parameters, metrics and model.

## Candidates

| Model | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Home team | Always picks home, at the training home win rate (56.8%) |
| Elo | The Elo win probability on its own ([Features](/docs/features)) |
| Logistic regression | All 56 features, standardized; regularization picked on validation (C = 0.1) |
| LightGBM | Tuned with Optuna (100 trials); settled on very shallow trees (5 leaves, 350 trees) |
| Ensemble | A log-odds blend of the two; its best weight was 0.9 logistic regression |

## Validation season (2024-25)

| Model | Log loss | Brier | Accuracy | AUC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home team | 0.6905 | 0.2487 | 54.4% | 0.500 |
| Elo | 0.6083 | 0.2107 | 66.4% | 0.723 |
| Logistic regression + Platt | **0.5955** | 0.2049 | 68.1% | 0.741 |
| LightGBM + Platt | 0.6001 | 0.2065 | 67.9% | 0.737 |
| Ensemble + Platt | 0.5956 | 0.2049 | 68.0% | 0.741 |

LightGBM never caught the linear model: with these features, the relationships are close to
additive in log-odds. The champion was the **calibrated logistic regression**, by the rule chosen
in advance: lowest validation log loss.

## Test season (2025-26), scored once

All models were refit on 2015-16 to 2024-25 (11,979 games) and scored on the 1,230 games of 2025-26.

| Model | Log loss | Brier | Accuracy | AUC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home team | 0.6874 | 0.2472 | 55.5% | 0.500 |
| Elo | **0.5978** | 0.2054 | 69.2% | 0.738 |
| Logistic regression + Platt | 0.6022 | 0.2073 | 67.1% | 0.733 |
| LightGBM + Platt | 0.6020 | 0.2071 | 68.1% | 0.735 |
| Ensemble + Platt | 0.6018 | 0.2072 | 67.1% | 0.734 |

A walk-forward run (refit every month on everything before it) agrees: Elo 0.598, logistic
regression 0.602.

## Reading it honestly

On the test season, plain Elo edged out the champion. The per-game log-loss gap is +0.004 with a
paired-bootstrap 95% interval of −0.004 to +0.011, so it isn't statistically meaningful; the two
models' probabilities correlate at 0.956, and the champion led by 0.013 on validation. Swapping in
Elo because of test numbers would turn the test season into a tuning set, so the champion stays,
and Elo is compared on live games nobody has seen, on the [performance page](/performance).

## In production

The champion is refit on every finished game since 2015-16 and registered in MLflow as
`tipoff-game@champion`; its calibrator is fit out of time on 2025-26. A weekly retrain that only promotes
a challenger beating the champion on the latest window is planned; for now it's refit by hand.
