> Coming in Phase 9. This page describes the plan; projected lines will appear on game pages and
> the results screen once they're built and beat their baseline.

## What it will predict

For each team's top 8 players by projected minutes (players averaging 10+ minutes over their last
10 games): **points, rebounds and assists**, each with an 80% range.

## Two stages

1. **Minutes:** from recent minutes, whether the player starts, rest, which teammates are out (more
   minutes for the rest), and blowout risk (the size of the Elo gap).
2. **Per-minute rates:** from rolling per-36 numbers, usage, the opponent's defensive rating and
   pace, and home or away.

Projection = minutes × rate. One LightGBM regressor per stat, plus quantile models (10th and 90th
percentiles) for the range.

## The bar

The baseline is each player's last-10 average. Projections ship only if they beat it on mean
absolute error for each stat, on a season the models never saw, with the same
[leakage rules](/docs/leakage) as the game model.
