Each game becomes one row of **61 features**, almost all of them **home minus away** differences.
Every value is computed only from games before that game's date ([Preventing leakage](/docs/leakage)).
They're built in `ml/src/tipoff/features/` and listed in `columns.py`.

## The groups

| Group | Features |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | Elo difference (with the home-court edge), Elo's own home probability, season-to-date net rating and point margin |
| Form | Last-5 and last-10 net rating and margin, last-10 win rate, exponentially weighted net rating (half-life about 7 games) |
| Style and efficiency | Offensive and defensive rating, pace, and the four factors (eFG%, turnover %, offensive rebound %, free-throw rate) for and against, season-to-date and last 10 |
| Schedule | Rest days, back-to-backs, 3 games in 4 nights, games in the last 7 days, travel distance, time zones crossed |
| Availability | Share of season minutes from rotation players who are out, and a flag for a top-2 scorer out |
| Lineup | Lineup strength: the recent rotation's minutes times each player's box-score rating |
| Injury report | Lineup strength redone with the official injury report, the minutes expected to be lost to it, and whether each team's report was in |
| Context | Point in the season, the home team's home record, the away team's road record, neutral site |

## Four factors

Computed from the base box score, each with an offensive (own) and defensive (opponents') version:

```text
eFG%   = (FGM + 0.5 × 3PM) / FGA
TOV%   = TOV / (FGA + 0.44 × FTA + TOV)
OREB%  = OREB / (OREB + opponent DREB)
FT rate = FTM / FGA
```

## Early-season priors

A team's season-to-date numbers are noisy in November. Every season-to-date and windowed stat is
blended with a prior:

```text
value = (n × current + k × prior) / (n + k)
```

where `n` is games played so far and the prior is the team's previous-season average, pulled a
third of the way toward that season's league average. `k` is 10 for season-to-date, 3 for last 10,
2 for last 5 and 5 for the weighted average. Early rows lean on the prior; late ones on this season.

## Elo

Elo is tuned on the training seasons only (a 300-combination grid, scored by Elo's own log loss):
**K = 15**, 60% of the rating carried into a new season, a margin-of-victory multiplier, and a
home-court edge that follows the league's home win rate over the previous two seasons (about 47 Elo
points on average, 0 at neutral sites). The rolling home edge beat every fixed value, matching the
drop in home-court advantage since 2019.

## Rest, travel and who's out

![Two charts. Left: home win rate rises with more rest than the opponent (about 47% at three fewer days, 64% at two more). Right: teams on the second night of a back-to-back won 44.6% of games, against 51.2% rested.](/docs/figures/rest_effects.webp)

- Travel is measured arena to arena. Time zones crossed compares both arenas' UTC offsets on the
  same date, so a daylight-saving change isn't counted as travel.
- "Out" means a rotation player (20+ minutes a game over the team's last 10) who didn't play in the
  team's previous game, unless his latest game was for another team (traded). That's exactly what's
  known on the morning of the game.
- Upcoming games go through the same code as past ones, so training and prediction can't drift apart.

## Lineup strength

Each player gets a rating: his Game Score (a box-score composite: points, shots, free throws,
rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, fouls and turnovers) per minute, averaged over his games with
weights that halve every 25 games, and pulled toward the league average as if he'd played 300
average minutes. A team's lineup strength is the sum, over everyone who played in its previous 5
games, of his average minutes in those games times his rating. It sees a star who has been out
(his recent minutes drop), a newcomer in the rotation, and depth, which the "who's out" flags only
partly capture. Across six held-out seasons it was worth more than any other single addition to Elo.

## The injury report

The NBA publishes an official injury report through the day: every listed player's status for
tonight (Out, Doubtful, Questionable, Probable, Available). The model uses the report that was
current at **9 AM Eastern** on game day, judged by when the file was actually published, and again
the one current at **5:45 PM** for the evening update ([Daily pipeline](/docs/pipeline)).

Each status comes with a chance of playing, measured on 2019-20 to 2023-24: Out 2%, Doubtful 7%,
Questionable 51%, Probable 87%. Lineup strength is then recomputed with each player's recent minutes
times his chance of playing; the minutes a listed player is expected to lose go to his teammates in
proportion, and a player listed as available after missing the last five games comes back at his
usual minutes. The feature is the difference from plain lineup strength, so a star ruled out costs
his team the gap between him and the players who take his minutes, and a player out for weeks costs
nothing new (his minutes are already gone from the rotation). A second feature counts the minutes
expected to be lost. Reports exist from 2019-20 on; before that, and when a team hasn't filed yet,
both are zero.

The champion doesn't use every feature: on top of Elo it uses rest and travel, availability, lineup
strength, the injury report, last-10 form and home and road records. Season-to-date ratings and the short-window
averages added nothing beyond Elo ([Models](/docs/models)).

Changing any definition bumps `FEATURES_VERSION`, which changes the `features_hash` stored with
every prediction.
