- Elo beats the chosen champion (LogReg) on this season. Per-game log-loss difference
  (LogReg − Elo) on the walk-forward predictions: +0.0039, paired bootstrap 95% CI
  [−0.0036, +0.0113], P(LogReg better) = 0.17. Their predictions correlate at 0.956.
  LogReg was better in Oct–Nov and worse from December on. On 2024-25 validation LogReg led by
  0.013. Taken together the two are statistically indistinguishable.

  would make 2025-26 a tuning set. Elo is tracked as the challenger on live 2026-27 games.
